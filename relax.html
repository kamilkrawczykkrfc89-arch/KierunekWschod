Wellness & Nature

Relax – Spa & Garden

The Sauna and Salt Cave Building

A repurposed outbuilding houses a comprehensive wellness center designed for year-round recovery. Immerse yourself in tranquility.

Sauna: A Finnish-style sauna accommodating up to 8 persons, including a dedicated interior chill-out area.

Salt Cave (Basement)

  • Zone 1: Equipped with a salt graduation tower (tężnia solna) for respiratory health.
  • Zone 2: A relaxation room featuring a professional massage chair and specialized lighting for reading and deep relaxation.

Outdoor Wellness

Wood-Fired Hot Tub: A traditional "Ruska balia" for up to 7 persons. Wood-heated for use in all seasons, including winter under the stars.

Firepit Area: A large, circular social area integrated with a garden swing and a designated spot for bonfires.

Culinary Hub

A restored former coal storage building converted into a culinary masterpiece. Includes a Brick Smokehouse for curing meats and a Pizza/Bread Oven for traditional baking.
